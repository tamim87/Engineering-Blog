# Vision

This is a personal engineering website with two purposes:

1. A professional portfolio.
2. An engineering blog.

The two reinforce each other: a project links to a technical case study, which
links to related articles. The site should read as one coherent body of work,
not two unrelated sites.

## What it communicates

- who the author is
- what they build and what they know
- how they approach engineering problems
- what they have worked on
- what they write about

Audience: recruiters, engineers, employers, collaborators and other developers.

## Principles

- Credibility comes from real work and real writing, not decoration. No skill
  ratings, fake statistics, badges, or "passionate developer" filler.
- Production-ready does not mean production-complex. Disciplined practices
  (testing, CI, security, accessibility, SEO, documentation) with the simplest
  architecture that meets actual requirements.
- Content is separate from presentation, and English/Bengali support must stay
  possible.
